# Tomato Target Spot

**Description:** Fungal disease (Corynespora cassiicola) favoured by warm, humid weather.

## Symptoms
- Brown lesions with concentric rings on leaves, stems and fruit.

## Actions
- Remove infected leaves.
- Improve airflow by pruning.

## Prevention
- Rotate crops and remove residue.
- Avoid prolonged leaf wetness.

## Warning
Fruit lesions spreading quickly need expert advice.
