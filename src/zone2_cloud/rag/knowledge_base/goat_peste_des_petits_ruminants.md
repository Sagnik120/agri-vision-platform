# Goat Peste Des Petits Ruminants

**Description:** Severe viral disease (PPR) of sheep and goats causing fever, sores in the mouth, and diarrhea.

**Safety Critical:** True

## Actions
- Quarantine new animals.
- Vaccinate flock.
- Provide supportive care and antibiotics for secondary infections.

## Warning
High mortality rate; notify vet services.
