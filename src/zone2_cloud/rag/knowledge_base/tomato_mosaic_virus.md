# Tomato Mosaic Virus

**Description:** Highly stable virus spread by contact, tools, hands and seed.

## Symptoms
- Light/dark green mottling, distorted fern-like leaves, uneven fruit ripening.

## Actions
- Remove infected plants.
- Wash hands and disinfect tools after handling.

## Prevention
- Use certified seed and resistant varieties.
- Avoid tobacco use near plants.

## Warning
Consult an expert if many plants show mottling.
