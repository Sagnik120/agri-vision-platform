# Grape Black Rot

**Description:** Fungal disease (Guignardia bidwellii) favoured by warm, wet weather.

## Symptoms
- Brown leaf spots with dark borders; berries shrivel into hard black mummies.

## Actions
- Remove mummified berries and infected leaves.
- Open canopy by pruning.

## Prevention
- Sanitation of vineyard debris.
- Train vines for airflow.

## Warning
Bunch infection spreading fast needs expert advice.
