# Tomato Leaf Mold

**Description:** Fungal disease (Passalora fulva) common in humid greenhouses and dense plantings.

## Symptoms
- Pale yellow spots on upper leaf surface with olive-green mould underneath.

## Actions
- Remove infected leaves.
- Increase ventilation and reduce humidity.

## Prevention
- Space and prune plants.
- Use resistant varieties.

## Warning
If the whole canopy is affected, consult an expert.
