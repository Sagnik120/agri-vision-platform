# Livestock Heat Stress

**Description:** Animal showing excessive panting, lethargy, and reduced feed intake due to high environmental temperatures.

**Safety Critical:** True

## Actions
- Move the animal to a shaded, well-ventilated area immediately.
- Provide access to abundant cool drinking water.
- Spray the animal with cool water or use fans.

## Warning
Severe heat stress can lead to fatal heat stroke. Monitor closely.
