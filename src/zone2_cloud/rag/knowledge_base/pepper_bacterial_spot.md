# Pepper Bacterial Spot

**Description:** Bacterial disease causing small, dark, raised spots on pepper leaves and fruit.

**Safety Critical:** False

## Actions
- Remove infected plant debris and avoid working with wet plants.
- Apply copper-based bactericide as a preventive measure.
- Use certified disease-free seed in future planting.
- Avoid overhead irrigation.

## Warning
Bacterial spot does not respond to fungicides — use copper-based bactericides only.
