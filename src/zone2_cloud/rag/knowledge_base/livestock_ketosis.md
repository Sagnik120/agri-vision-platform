# Livestock Ketosis

**Description:** Metabolic disorder usually occurring in high-yielding dairy cows shortly after calving, causing sweet-smelling breath and weight loss.

**Safety Critical:** False

## Actions
- Provide high-energy feed supplements (e.g., propylene glycol or molasses).
- Ensure access to high-quality forage.
- Consult a veterinarian if the animal refuses to eat.

## Warning
Can lead to severe milk drop and liver damage if untreated.
