# Cherry Powdery Mildew

**Description:** Fungal disease forming white growth on young leaves and fruit.

## Symptoms
- White powdery patches on leaves; curled, distorted young leaves.

## Actions
- Prune to improve airflow.
- Remove heavily infected shoots.

## Prevention
- Avoid excess nitrogen.
- Manage irrigation to limit humidity.

## Warning
Fruit infection needs expert advice.
