# Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus

**Description:** Viral disease transmitted by whiteflies; no cure once infected.

## Symptoms
- Upward curling, yellowing leaf margins, stunted plants, poor fruit set.

## Actions
- Uproot and destroy infected plants early.
- Control whiteflies with sticky traps.

## Prevention
- Use resistant varieties and insect-proof nursery nets.
- Remove weed hosts.

## Warning
Spread to many plants needs expert advice on vector control.
