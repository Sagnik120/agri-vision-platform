# Peach Bacterial Spot

**Description:** Bacterial disease (Xanthomonas arboricola) spread by wind-driven rain.

## Symptoms
- Small angular purple-brown leaf spots that fall out ('shot holes'), pitted fruit.

## Actions
- Prune infected twigs.
- Avoid working in wet orchards.

## Prevention
- Plant tolerant varieties.
- Maintain tree vigour.

## Warning
Severe fruit damage needs expert advice.
