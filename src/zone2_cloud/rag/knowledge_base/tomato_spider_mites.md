# Tomato Two-Spotted Spider Mite

**Description:** Tiny mite pest thriving in hot, dry conditions.

## Symptoms
- Fine yellow stippling on leaves, webbing on the underside, bronzed dry leaves.

## Actions
- Spray plants with water to dislodge mites.
- Remove heavily infested leaves.

## Prevention
- Avoid water stress.
- Conserve natural predators by limiting broad-spectrum sprays.

## Warning
Severe webbing across the field needs expert advice.
