# Tomato Septoria Leaf Spot

**Description:** Fungal disease (Septoria lycopersici) spread by rain splash on lower leaves.

## Symptoms
- Many small circular spots with dark borders and grey centres, often with tiny black dots.

## Actions
- Remove infected lower leaves.
- Mulch to reduce soil splash.

## Prevention
- Rotate crops and remove debris.
- Water at the base of plants.

## Warning
Heavy defoliation needs expert advice.
