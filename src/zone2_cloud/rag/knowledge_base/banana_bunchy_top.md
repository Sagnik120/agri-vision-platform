# Banana Bunchy Top

**Description:** Devastating viral disease causing stunted growth and bunching of leaves at the top of the banana plant.

**Safety Critical:** True

## Actions
- Uproot and completely destroy the infected plant to prevent spread.
- Control aphid populations which spread the virus.
- Use certified virus-free planting material in the future.

## Warning
Highly infectious and cannot be cured. Eradicate infected plants immediately.
