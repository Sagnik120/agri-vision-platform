# Mastitis Suspected

**Description:** Suspected udder inflammation, often from bacterial infection, reducing milk quality/yield.

**Safety Critical:** False

## Actions
- Milk the affected quarter separately and discard the milk.
- Keep the animal in a clean, dry area.
- Contact a veterinarian for antibiotic/anti-inflammatory guidance.
- Check milking equipment hygiene to prevent spread to other animals.

## Warning
Untreated mastitis can become chronic — seek veterinary care promptly.
