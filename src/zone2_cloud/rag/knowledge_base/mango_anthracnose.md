# Mango Anthracnose

**Description:** Fungal disease causing dark irregular spots on leaves, blossoms, and fruits of mango.

**Safety Critical:** False

## Actions
- Apply fungicides during flowering and early fruit set.
- Prune canopy for better aeration.

## Warning
Can cause severe post-harvest rot.
