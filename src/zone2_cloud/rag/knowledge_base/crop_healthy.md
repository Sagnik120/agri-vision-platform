# Healthy Crop

**Description:** No disease symptoms detected on the photographed leaf.

## Symptoms
- Uniform green colour, no spots, lesions, mould or curling.

## Actions
- No treatment needed.
- Continue routine weekly scouting.

## Prevention
- Maintain crop rotation, clean tools and balanced fertilisation.

## Warning
Re-check if new spots, wilting or discolouration appear.
