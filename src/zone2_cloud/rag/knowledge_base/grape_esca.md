# Grape Esca (Black Measles)

**Description:** Trunk disease complex caused by wood-decay fungi.

## Symptoms
- Tiger-striped leaves, dark spotting on berries, sudden vine collapse.

## Actions
- Mark affected vines and prune out dead wood.
- Disinfect pruning tools.

## Prevention
- Prune in dry weather and protect large cuts.
- Remove dead vines.

## Warning
Sudden collapse of vines needs expert advice.
