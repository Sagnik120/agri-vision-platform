# Tomato Bacterial Spot

**Description:** Bacterial disease (Xanthomonas) spread by rain splash, tools and infected seed.

## Symptoms
- Small dark water-soaked spots on leaves and raised scabby spots on fruit.

## Actions
- Remove infected plants/leaves.
- Avoid handling plants when wet.

## Prevention
- Use certified disease-free seed and transplants.
- Rotate crops; avoid overhead irrigation.

## Warning
Widespread fruit spotting needs expert advice.
