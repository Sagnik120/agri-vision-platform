# Grape Leaf Blight (Isariopsis Leaf Spot)

**Description:** Fungal leaf disease causing premature defoliation in humid conditions.

## Symptoms
- Irregular dark brown spots on leaves that dry and drop.

## Actions
- Remove infected leaves.
- Improve canopy airflow.

## Prevention
- Clear fallen leaves.
- Avoid overhead irrigation.

## Warning
Heavy defoliation needs expert advice.
