# Apple Black Rot

**Description:** Fungal disease (Botryosphaeria obtusa) entering through wounds and dead wood.

## Symptoms
- Purple-bordered leaf spots ('frog-eye'), black rotting fruit, cankers on branches.

## Actions
- Prune out dead wood and cankers.
- Remove mummified fruit.

## Prevention
- Keep orchard sanitation.
- Avoid tree wounds.

## Warning
Large branch cankers need expert advice.
