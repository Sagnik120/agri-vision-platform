# Squash Powdery Mildew

**Description:** Fungal disease of cucurbits producing white powdery growth, favoured by dry days and humid nights.

## Symptoms
- White powdery patches on upper leaf surfaces; leaves yellow and dry.

## Actions
- Remove heavily infected leaves.
- Improve spacing and airflow.

## Prevention
- Plant tolerant varieties.
- Avoid excess nitrogen.

## Warning
If most leaves are covered, consult an agriculture officer.
