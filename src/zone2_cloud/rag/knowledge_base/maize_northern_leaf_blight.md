# Maize Northern Leaf Blight

**Description:** Fungal disease (Exserohilum turcicum) spreading in cool, humid, dewy weather.

## Symptoms
- Long cigar-shaped grey-green to tan lesions on leaves.

## Actions
- Scout lower leaves after humid spells.
- Remove severely affected leaves.

## Prevention
- Rotate crops and manage residue.
- Use resistant hybrids.

## Warning
Rapid spread on upper leaves before tasselling needs expert advice.
