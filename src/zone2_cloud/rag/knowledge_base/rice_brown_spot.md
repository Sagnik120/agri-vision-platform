# Rice Brown Spot

**Description:** Fungal disease (Bipolaris oryzae) often linked to nutrient-poor or stressed soils.

## Symptoms
- Oval brown spots with grey centres on leaves and grains.

## Actions
- Correct nutrient deficiencies after a soil test.
- Remove infected stubble after harvest.

## Prevention
- Use healthy, treated seed.
- Maintain balanced fertilisation.

## Warning
Grain discolouration across the field needs expert advice.
