# Rice Blast

**Description:** Devastating fungal disease causing diamond-shaped lesions on rice leaves.

**Safety Critical:** True

## Actions
- Maintain proper flooding in paddies.
- Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilization.
- Apply tricyclazole or similar fungicide.

## Warning
Highly destructive; treat early.
