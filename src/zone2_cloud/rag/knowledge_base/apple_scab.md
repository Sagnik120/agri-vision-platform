# Apple Scab

**Description:** Fungal disease (Venturia inaequalis) favoured by cool, wet spring weather.

## Symptoms
- Olive-green to black velvety spots on leaves and scabby lesions on fruit.

## Actions
- Collect and destroy fallen leaves.
- Prune for open canopy.

## Prevention
- Plant resistant varieties.
- Remove leaf litter in autumn.

## Warning
Severe fruit scabbing needs expert advice on protective sprays.
