# Abnormal Temperature

**Description:** Sensor detected temperature outside the normal range for cattle, combined with behavioural signs.

**Safety Critical:** False

## Actions
- Recheck temperature with a manual thermometer to confirm sensor reading.
- Ensure adequate shade, water and ventilation.
- Monitor feed intake and activity over the next 12 hours.
- Contact a veterinarian if fever persists beyond 24 hours.

## Warning
Persistent high fever combined with low feed intake needs urgent veterinary attention.
