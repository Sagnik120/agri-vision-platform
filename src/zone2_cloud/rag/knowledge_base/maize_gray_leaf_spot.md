# Maize Gray Leaf Spot (Cercospora)

**Description:** Fungal leaf disease favoured by warm, humid weather and long leaf wetness.

## Symptoms
- Narrow rectangular grey-to-tan lesions running parallel between leaf veins.

## Actions
- Remove heavily infected lower leaves where practical.
- Improve field airflow and avoid dense planting.

## Prevention
- Rotate with non-cereal crops.
- Bury or remove infected crop residue.
- Use tolerant hybrids.

## Warning
If lesions reach the ear leaf before grain fill, consult an agriculture officer.
