# Citrus Greening (Huanglongbing)

**Description:** Serious bacterial disease spread by the Asian citrus psyllid; no cure.

## Symptoms
- Blotchy asymmetric yellow mottling of leaves, small lopsided bitter fruit, twig dieback.

## Actions
- Report suspected trees to the horticulture department.
- Remove confirmed infected trees.

## Prevention
- Plant certified disease-free saplings.
- Monitor and control psyllids.

## Warning
Suspected greening should always be confirmed by an expert.
