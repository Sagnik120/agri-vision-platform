# Strawberry Leaf Scorch

**Description:** Fungal disease (Diplocarpon earlianum) favoured by warm, wet weather.

## Symptoms
- Many small purple spots merging so leaves look scorched.

## Actions
- Remove infected leaves after harvest.
- Improve spacing.

## Prevention
- Use disease-free planting material.
- Renovate beds regularly.

## Warning
Consult an expert if most leaves are scorched.
