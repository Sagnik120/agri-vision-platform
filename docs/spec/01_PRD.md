# Agri-Vision PRD

## Problem Statement
Rural farmers lack a unified platform for crop and livestock management. High network latency, low English literacy, and complex UIs prevent adoption.

## Goals
- **Unified App:** One app for crop diseases and livestock health.
- **Offline-First:** High-confidence inferences run entirely on-device (Edge AI).
- **Bilingual & Accessible:** English/Hindi interface with a simple photo-first flow and typed symptom description.
- **Cloud Escalation:** Low-confidence inferences escalate to Gemini and localized RAG.

## Non-Goals
- Real-time video streaming processing.
- Direct IoT integration in the initial prototype (sensors are simulated).
- Replacing human veterinarians entirely (system acts as an advisory and escalation tool).
